Delete Your Account

Astro Report by Astro Animation Studio

You can ask us to delete your Astro Report account and the data linked to it at any time. You do not need to reinstall the app to do this.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to support@astroanimationstudio.com from the email address linked to your account, or include your username in the email.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Astro Report account
  3. Include your username so we can find your account.
  4. We may reply to confirm that you own the account.
  5. We will delete your account within 7 days and email you when it is done.
Email deletion request

What data is deleted

DataWhat happens
Account details (username, password, email)Permanently deleted
All weekly reports you createdPermanently deleted
Report files and downloads stored on our serverPermanently deleted

What data may be kept

  • Server backups: copies of your data in backups are removed automatically within 30 days.
  • Server security logs (such as IP address and login time) are kept for up to 30 days and then deleted.
  • We do not keep any other data after your account is deleted.
Note: Deletion is permanent. Download any reports you want to keep before you send the request.

Advertising data

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google handles ad-related data under its own policy. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads.
